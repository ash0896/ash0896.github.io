Jul 2022 – May 2026

Ph.D. Researcher

Max Planck Institute for Chemical Energy Conversion · Group of Prof. Dr. Serena DeBeer, Dept. of Inorganic Spectroscopy, Mülheim an der Ruhr, Germany

  • Consortium research: Led two collaborative biocatalysis projects on LPMOs within the CUBE consortium.
  • Metalloenzyme mechanism: Used QM/MM (DFT) and Local Energy Decomposition (LED) calculations to dissect how second-sphere residues modulate O₂ and H₂O₂ activation in LPMOs (two manuscripts in preparation).
  • Electronic structure & spectroscopy: Combined HERFD-XAS with TDDFT to dissect first- and second-sphere effects in Cu(I) LPMOs (submitted, Inorg. Chem.); supported interpretation of Cu K-edge XAS data for collaborative LPMOs (Inorg. Chem. 2024).
  • Model complex reactivity: Contributed computational/electronic-structure analysis to studies of a diiron complex's formation pathways and reactivity (JACS 2026).
  • Structural/electronic modulation: Investigated structural and electronic changes in LPMOs upon chitin binding (Chem. Sci. 2025); identified a lysine–cysteine redox switch with a NOS bridge in transaldolase via S K-edge XAS and TDDFT (J. Phys. Chem. Lett. 2024).
  • Workflow development: Designed and automated reproducible QM/MM, DFT, and free-energy pipelines on HPC/SLURM systems using Python and Bash.
